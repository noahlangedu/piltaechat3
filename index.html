Privacy & safety

What information is used?

PILTAE CHAT uses your email address for account authentication. Your account ID, display name, messages, and moderation records are used to operate the service, display chat messages, protect the community, and enforce moderation.

Is my password visible to chat members?

No. This website sends passwords to Supabase Authentication for sign-up and sign-in. It does not put passwords in chat messages or the public messages table. Use a unique password and never send it to anyone.

Will information be stolen or sold?

PILTAE CHAT is intended to use account information only to operate and secure the chat. This page does not intentionally collect payment details or request access to your other accounts. However, a privacy notice cannot guarantee that information will never be exposed, and this website cannot independently guarantee how every infrastructure provider processes data. Review Supabase's privacy and security documentation for its services.

Who can read my messages?

Messages are not private direct messages. Authenticated chat members can read messages. Do not post passwords, financial information, home addresses, or other sensitive data. Administrators may review chat information to moderate the community.

Cookies and sessions

Supabase's client library maintains an authentication session so you do not need to sign in after every page action. Do not stay signed in on a shared computer.

Data retention and account requests

Messages and moderation records may remain in the database until the service owner deletes them or implements a retention policy. Contact the site owner to request assistance with account or data deletion. Account deletion should be handled through a secure administrative process.

This notice describes the intended operation of PILTAE CHAT; it is not a guarantee against breaches or a substitute for reviewing applicable legal requirements.
