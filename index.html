Privacy & safety

What information is used?

Your email is used for authentication. Your account ID, display name, messages, and moderation records are used to operate the service and protect the community.

Are passwords visible to chat members?

No. Passwords are submitted to Supabase Authentication and are not put into chat messages or the public messages table. Use a unique password and never share it.

Will information be stolen or sold?

PILTAE CHAT is intended to use account information to provide the chat and its moderation features. This website does not request payment details or access to your other accounts. However, no online service can guarantee that information will never be exposed. Review your provider's privacy and security documentation.

Who can read messages?

Messages are not private direct messages. Authenticated members can read chat messages, and authorized administrators may review information to moderate the community. Never post passwords, financial details, or other sensitive information.

Sessions and data retention

Your login session is maintained so you do not need to sign in after every action. Log out on shared devices. Messages and moderation records may remain stored until the owner deletes them or establishes a retention policy. Contact the site owner for data requests.

This notice describes the intended operation of PILTAE CHAT; it is not a guarantee against breaches or a substitute for reviewing applicable legal requirements.
